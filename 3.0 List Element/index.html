Angela's Recipe for the Best Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • 3/4 cup of warm milk
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg plus 1 egg yolk
  • 1/4 cup of butter
  • 3 cups of bread flour

For the filling:

  • 2/3 cup of a soft brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons of ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup of butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the milk with the yeast, sugar, and eggs.
  2. Melt the butter and add to the mixture.
  3. Add in the flour and mix until smooth.
  4. Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  5. Let the dough rise for 2 hours.
  6. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  7. Bake the dough for 30 minutes.
